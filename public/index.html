GAME OBJECTIVE

Legends Shootout is an instant ice hockey penalty shot between an NHL Alumni shooter and goalie. Pick the matchup or tap the shuffle button, place your bets and press Play. The shot is decided the moment you play.

Playing the game

  1. Choose a shooter and a goalie, or shuffle for a random matchup.
  2. Make your bet selections.
  3. Press Play and watch the shot.
  4. Pick a new matchup or re-bet the same one.

BET MARKETS

  • Match Result: the shooter scores, or the goalie saves. A miss counts as a save.
  • Score + Shot Type: a goal is scored with a forehand or a backhand.
  • Score + Target Area: a goal is scored in the top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right or five hole. Areas are seen from the shooter's side.
  • Score + Shot Type + Target Area: the shot type and target area of the goal are both correct.
  • Save + Save Type: no goal is scored and the goalie stops it with the glove, blocker, pad or stick, or the shooter misses the net.

Score bets lose if no goal is scored. Save bets lose if a goal is scored.

AUTO PLAY

Auto Play places bets and plays rounds for you until the number of rounds, the loss limit or the single win limit is reached.

GENERAL INFORMATION

  • A random number generator decides every shot.
  • Malfunction voids all plays and pays.
  • Bets can't be cancelled once played.
  • Minimum bet, maximum bet and maximum payout are set by the operator.
  • Changing the matchup reprices any selections on the betslip.

RTP (RETURN TO PLAYER)

RTP: 90% on every selection, before odds are rounded down to two decimals. RTP is a long-run average and is not guaranteed over a short run of play.
